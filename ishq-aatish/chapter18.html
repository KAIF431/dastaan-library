⏱️ 2 Min Read • 217 Words • Author: Sadiya Rajpoot

Is par khull padi red cover ki diary uthai aur isi terhan chalte study table tak aagaye.Diary ko daraaz mein rakh kar daraaz ko lock kiya aur chaabi haath mein le li phir Taniya ko mukhatib kiye baghair kaha."Mein fresh ho kar aata hoon."Aur kamrey mein baney attached baath room mein chaley gaye. Taniya ne ghair dilchaspi se yahan wahan sar ghumaya, phir thodhee ke neechey haath rakh kar aatish daan ke oopar lagi tasweer ko dekhne lagi.Kuch hi der baad Azhar Farooqi baath room ka darwaaza khol kar kamre mein aagaye."Aao chalein."Unhon ne kaha.

Taniya foran uth khadi hui.Sath sath dono ko dinning room ke darwazey se aata dekh kar Noor ul huda ka muh ki taraf jata haath wahi ruk gaya.Unhone niwala plate mein rakha aur ajeeb si nazron se Azhar Farooqi ko dekhney lagey.Kursi khech kar inhen bithate hue Taniya ne apne papa ko dekha to thitak gayi.Doosri taraf Noor ul huda ne iski nigahon ko mehsoos kar ke ankhen jhukate hue plate se niwala utha kar muh mein rakha magar usey nighalney ke liye inhen pani ka sahara lena pada tha.Taniya qasdan inhen nazar andaaz kar ke khud bhi baith gayi.

Azhar Farooqi ki plate mein khana nikalte hue itratein hue Maryam se kaha."Dekha mamma! Mein ne kaha to dada jaan foran aagaye.
