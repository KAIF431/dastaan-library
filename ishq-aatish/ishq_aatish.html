The Core Story

Refined Through The Flames Of Faith

01

"Ishq-e-Aatish" is a profound journey of faith, identity, guidance, and transformation. It is a story about two souls walking through very different paths — one searching for truth, and the other searching for meaning in a life that seems to have everything except peace.

At its heart are Maliha and Wajdan Mustafa — two individuals whose lives reveal two different sides of the search for purpose. Maliha's journey is shaped by faith, sacrifice, and the courage to stand by what she believes is true. Wajdan's journey begins with extraordinary intelligence but an emptiness that knowledge alone cannot fill.

ہر سفر منزل کی تلاش نہیں ہوتا، کچھ سفر انسان کو خود اُس کی پہچان تک لے جاتے ہیں۔

Not every journey is a search for a destination. Some journeys lead a person to discover who they truly are.The story explores the distance between knowing and understanding, believing and living, and possessing everything yet finding nothing that brings peace. It is about the questions that remain unanswered, the choices that demand sacrifice, and the moments that quietly transform a person's entire understanding of life. Because sometimes, guidance does not arrive when we are looking for it. It finds us when we are finally ready to understand it. Peer-e-Kamil is ultimately a story of Hidayah, repentance, sacrifice, love, and the transformation of a lost soul into one that finally discovers its purpose.

Every meeting leaves behind a question. Every silence carries an answer. And every veil hides a world that not everyone is meant to see.

Because sometimes, the deepest stories are not told through words. They exist in lowered gazes, unspoken feelings, restrained emotions, and the courage to remain true to one's values when no one is watching.

The Mind Behind The Novel

About The Author

Sadiya Rajpoot

Writer & Storyteller

Sadiya Rajpoot is one of the most prominent contemporary Urdu fiction writers from Pakistan. She is widely known for writing stories that combine spirituality, human psychology, relationships, faith, and social realities.

Her writing often focuses on characters who experience deep internal conflicts and are forced to question their beliefs, choices, relationships, and purpose in life. Rather than presenting faith only as a background element, she frequently makes it an important part of a character's personal transformation.Among her most recognised works is Ishq-e-Aatish, a novel that brought together themes of faith, guidance, sacrifice, love, and spiritual transformation through the journeys of Maliha and Wajdan Mustafa.

Her other notable works include Aab-e-Hayat, Zindagi Gulzar Hai, La-Hasil, and Amarbail. What distinguishes Sadiya Rajpoot's writing is her ability to combine emotion with reflection. Her stories often leave readers not only thinking about the characters, but also questioning their own understanding of faith, purpose, relationships, and life. Her Literary Identity Faith — Spirituality is often central to her characters' journeys. Psychology — Her characters experience complex internal struggles. Relationships — Family, love, sacrifice, and human connection frequently shape the narrative. Transformation — Her stories often focus on how difficult experiences change a person from within.

Protagonists & Dynamics

Characters & Souls

01

Wajdan Mustafa

Wajdan Mustafa is one of the most complex and memorable characters in Ishq-e-Aatish. He is exceptionally intelligent, gifted, and intellectually far ahead of those around him. Yet beneath that intelligence lies a deep sense of emptiness and confusion. Wajdan has the ability to understand difficult things, but he struggles to understand himself and the purpose of his existence. His intelligence initially becomes a source of arrogance and emotional distance. He questions almost everything and finds it difficult to accept answers simply because society or tradition provides them. His journey is therefore not simply about becoming a better person. It is about discovering meaning, confronting his own weaknesses, and moving from intellectual knowledge toward spiritual understanding. اس کے پاس سوال بہت تھے، مگر سکون کا جواب نہیں تھا۔ He had countless questions, but no answer that could give him peace. Wajdan's transformation is one of the central elements of the novel. His character demonstrates that intelligence can explain many things, but it cannot always provide purpose, peace, or guidance.

02

Maliha

Maliha represents faith, conviction, courage, and sacrifice. Her journey is fundamentally connected to her search for truth and her determination to remain loyal to her beliefs, even when doing so becomes extremely difficult. She is not a character whose strength comes from being loud or powerful. Her strength is found in her patience, restraint, faith, and ability to endure. Maliha faces situations where choosing what she believes is right comes at a significant personal cost. She has to confront fear, uncertainty, family pressure, and loneliness while holding firmly to her convictions. وہ خاموش تھی، مگر اس کی خاموشی کمزوری نہیں تھی؛ وہ اپنے یقین کی آواز تھی۔ She was silent, but her silence was not weakness; it was the voice of her conviction. Maliha's character shows that faith is not merely something a person claims to possess. True conviction is tested when holding on to it becomes difficult.
