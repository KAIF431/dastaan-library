A poignant tale of emotional resilience, destiny, and spiritual growth following Wajdan and Maliha as they navigate tests of faith and love.

SYNOPSIS

Beneath The Veil Of Silence

Ishq-e-Aatish follows the lives of two distinct individuals, Wajdan and Maliha. Through trial and self-discovery, the narrative demonstrates how suffering refines character like fire purifies gold.

AUTHOR

Sadiya Rajpoot

Sadiya Rajpoot is a celebrated Urdu author known for her compelling narratives, emotional nuance, and deep exploration of human relationships.

CHARACTERS

Characters & Souls

Wajdan

A resilient protagonist searching for purpose and internal peace through life's trials.

Maliha

A strong-willed heroine whose journey is marked by faith, patience, and devotion.
